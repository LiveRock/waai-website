## Submission checklist (each directory)

1. Create account with a business email (suggest `peter@hotsource.net` or `hello@waai.me` — consistent everywhere)
2. Pick the tier-appropriate variant; reword the first sentence
3. Upload logo (1024×1024), up to 5 screenshots, link demo video
4. Link to `https://waai.me` (homepage) unless a deeper page fits better (pricing for review sites)
5. Log it in the tracker: date, status, account email
6. Once live, verify the backlink: `curl -s <listing-url> | grep -i 'rel='` — if the waai.me link has no `nofollow`/`ugc`, it's dofollow ✅

Every WhatsApp automation platform promises to "automate your business." Most demos look identical: a friendly bot, a quick reply, a happy customer. The differences only show up weeks later — when you need a real booking flow with deposits and staff schedules, when you want your data in your own hands, or when a client asks you to white-label the whole thing.

So before you sign up for anything, here's the framework we'd use. Evaluate any WhatsApp chatbot builder against these seven criteria, and the right choice becomes obvious.

## TL;DR

- **Builder depth matters more than builder beauty.** You need conditions, variables, branching, and loops — not just a pretty drag-and-drop canvas.
- **Rule-based matching first, LLM fallback second** beats pure AI for reliability and cost.
- **Booking is the killer use case.** Multi-branch support, calendars, and reminders separate serious platforms from toys.
- **Bring-your-own AI model** gives you control over cost, quality, and privacy.
- **Own your data and your number.** If a platform locks you in, walk away.
- **Flat, transparent pricing** beats per-message surprises.
- **A reseller program** is essential if you're an agency — Meta's free in-app Business Agent has none at all.

## The 7 criteria that actually matter

### 1. No-code builder depth

Anyone can build a "reply to keywords" bot. The question is: what happens when your flow needs logic? Look for a visual builder with a real step library — conditions, variables, branching, loops — not just message-and-button blocks. waai's flow builder includes 30+ step types, which is enough to model genuinely complex business processes without writing code. If a platform caps out at "send message, ask question, send another message," it will hit a wall the moment your business logic gets real.

Also check the AI side. The best architecture uses **rule-based (fuzzy) matching first**, with an LLM fallback for everything the rules can't handle. That gives you predictable, cheap, fast answers for common questions and flexible AI only where it's needed. Pure-LLM bots are slow, expensive, and occasionally creative in ways you won't enjoy.

### 2. Booking capability

For most service businesses — salons, clinics, studios, agencies — booking is the number-one automation win. But not all booking is equal. A serious system handles:

- Multiple branches, staff, and services
- Holidays and working hours
- Deposits and reminders
- Google Calendar two-way sync
- PDF receipts

waai's multi-branch booking system covers all of the above. Meta's free in-app Business Agent, by contrast, does not do booking systems like this at all — it's a FAQ responder. If bookings are your revenue, this criterion alone should decide your choice.

### 3. Integrations

Your chatbot shouldn't be an island. Look for native integrations with the tools you already run: Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, and Sheets at minimum, plus a contacts CRM for storing customer context. waai ships with Google Workspace integration and a built-in CRM with broadcast campaigns and message templates, so conversations turn into records and records turn into revenue.

### 4. AI model choice

Here's a question most buyers never ask: *whose AI model is running my bot?* Many platforms lock you into one provider. waai is bring-your-own: connect OpenAI, Anthropic, z.ai, llama.cpp, or OpenRouter. That means you control cost, latency, quality, and where your data goes. It also means you're not hostage to a single vendor's price changes.

### 5. Data ownership and infrastructure

Two things to verify: (a) do you get **your own WhatsApp number** built on the official WhatsApp Business API — waai is a verified Meta Tech Provider — and (b) who owns the conversation data? With Meta's free in-app agent, you're inside Meta's ecosystem with its limits. With waai, you get your own number and your own data. For any business that treats customer conversations as an asset, this is non-negotiable.

### 6. Pricing transparency

Per-message pricing turns your success into your penalty: the more customers you serve, the more you pay. Prefer flat monthly pricing with clear plan tiers. waai offers Trial, Starter, Pro, and Custom plans at flat monthly rates — you can budget exactly what automation costs, and scaling your message volume doesn't scale your invoice.

### 7. Reseller and agency option

If you're an agency, ask one question: *can I resell this under my own brand with recurring revenue?* Meta's free Business Agent has no reseller program at all. Most platforms don't either. waai has a white-label reseller/agency program with recurring commission — you sell automation under your brand, and the revenue compounds month after month.

## How to run your own evaluation

Whichever platforms you shortlist, stress-test them with the same five questions:

1. **Show me a flow with a conditional branch and a loop.** If they can't demo it live, it doesn't exist.
2. **Book an appointment for a business with two locations, three staff, and a deposit.** Watch what breaks.
3. **Which AI models can I connect, and can I swap them later?**
4. **Export my data and take my number with me — what happens?**
5. **What's my total cost at 10x my current message volume?** Flat or not?

Platforms that answer all five confidently are worth shortlisting. waai answers yes to all of them — which is exactly why we built it.

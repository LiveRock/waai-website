## Why WhatsApp is the right channel for patient communication

Think about how patients actually behave. A toothache at 9pm doesn't turn into a phone call to your answering machine — it turns into a WhatsApp message. If nobody answers until morning, the patient has already booked with the competitor who replied at 9:02pm.

WhatsApp offers something email and phone can't match in healthcare:

- **Near-universal adoption.** Across much of the world, WhatsApp is the default messaging app, so there's nothing for patients to install or learn.
- **Asynchronous by nature.** Patients can ask a question at midnight and get an instant, accurate answer — no hold music, no missed calls.
- **Low friction.** No portals to log into, no forms to hunt for. One conversation handles the question, the booking, and the reminder.
- **Written record.** Instructions, addresses, and confirmations live in the chat where patients can find them again.

The problem, of course, is that a human answering every message doesn't scale. That's the gap AI automation fills.

## Reminders that cut no-shows

No-shows are the quiet killer of clinic revenue. An empty chair at 2pm isn't just awkward — it's an unrecoverable slot that another patient would have happily taken.

The remedy is unglamorous and effective: timely, well-crafted reminders. Automating them on WhatsApp means:

- **Confirmation immediately after booking**, so the appointment is anchored.
- **A reminder 24–48 hours before**, with an easy one-tap way to confirm or reschedule.
- **A final nudge a few hours ahead**, reducing same-day no-shows.
- **Automatic reschedule handling**, so a cancellation instantly frees the slot in your calendar instead of requiring a phone tag session.

Because it's all driven from your booking flows, reminders go out consistently for every practitioner, every branch, every service — not just when the front desk remembers.

## Triage questions and pre-visit automation

Clinics ask patients questions before every visit: medications, allergies, symptoms, forms to bring. Doing this by phone wastes staff time; doing it by paper wastes chair time.

With waai's visual logic-flow builder (30+ step types, including conditions, variables, and branching), you can build flows that:

- Ask structured pre-appointment questions and store answers in the patient's contact record.
- Route urgent-sounding symptoms to a "call us now" path with your emergency line, instead of letting the AI improvise medical advice.
- Send pre-visit instructions, directions, and preparation steps automatically.

One important principle: **an AI assistant should never diagnose or advise on treatment.** Design your flows so anything clinical escalates to a human. Automate the logistics — booking, reminders, FAQs, forms — and let your clinical team handle the medicine. That boundary protects patients and keeps you on the right side of professional guidelines.

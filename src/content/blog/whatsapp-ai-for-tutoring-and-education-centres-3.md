## Why WhatsApp is the front door for education businesses

Think about how a parent actually finds a tutor. They see a recommendation in a local group, they tap the number, and they message. No forms, no email, no "we'll get back to you." Same for adult learners booking professional training, or edtech companies supporting students at scale.

WhatsApp works for education because:

- **It's where the conversation already happens.** Parents forward your number to other parents. That's free, high-trust referral marketing.
- **Speed decides enrolments.** Enquiry windows are short. A family comparing three centres usually signs with the one that replied first, with the clearest answers.
- **Messages are low-friction.** A busy parent can answer one WhatsApp message in the school run. Getting them to fill a form is another story.

The problem, of course, is that most centres answer WhatsApp manually — between lessons, in the evenings, on weekends. Which is exactly when enquiries flood in.

An AI auto-responder on your own WhatsApp Business number can instantly answer the questions that make up 80% of your enquiries: subjects and levels offered, tutor availability, fees, locations, trial lesson policy, term dates. ### 2. Multi-branch booking (the big one)

This is where generic chatbots fall apart and waai shines. Education businesses rarely sell one thing at one time in one place. You have multiple branches, multiple tutors, multiple subjects, holiday breaks, deposits for term enrolments, and reminder schedules before every lesson.

waai's multi-branch booking system handles all of it:

Parents book themselves, right in the chat. Your admin team stops being a scheduling switchboard.

### 3. FAQ automation beyond the basics

"What should my child bring to the first lesson?" "Do you offer online sessions?" "How do I pay?" With waai's visual logic-flow builder — 30+ step types including conditions, variables, branching and loops — you can build flows that do more than answer: collect the student's year group, route GCSE vs A-Level enquiries differently, and hand complex cases to a human with full context attached.

For centres serving diverse communities, language is often the deciding trust factor. waai supports **5 locales**, so a parent can enquire in their preferred language and get a clear, correct answer — not a machine-translated guess. Every enquiry becomes a contact in your CRM. That means when enrolment season opens, you can run broadcast campaigns and message templates to re-engage last term's enquiries or notify parents about new classes — without exporting spreadsheets.

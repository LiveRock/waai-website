## 1. Depth of the No-Code Builder

The heart of any good automation platform is its logic engine. A simple "if this, then that" sequence works for basic queries, but real conversations are messy. Customers ask questions in different ways, change their minds mid-flow, and need to be steered toward specific outcomes.

You need a visual logic-flow builder that allows for complexity without requiring you to write code.

**Look for:**
*   **Fuzzy Matching:** The ability to understand intent even if the user makes a typo or phrases things oddly.
*   **LLM Fallback:** A safety net where, if the rule-based logic doesn't have an answer, a Large Language Model (like GPT-4) steps in to prevent the conversation from dead-ending.
*   **Advanced Steps:** Can you create loops? Can you set and manipulate variables? Can you branch the conversation based on user input?

At waai, we built a visual canvas with over 30 step types. This lets you design flows that mimic human logic. You can capture data, store it in variables, and use it later—crucial for personalized experiences. We prioritize rule-based matching first for speed and cost-efficiency, with LLM fallback as the safety net, ensuring your bot is both fast and smart.

## 2. Real Booking vs. Just "Sending a Link"

This is where many platforms fall short. They claim to do "booking," but all they actually do is send the user a Calendly or Acuity link. That forces the user to leave WhatsApp, open a browser, and potentially get distracted. It’s a friction point.

True automation keeps the user inside the app.

**Look for:**
*   **Native Booking System:** Can the user select a service, choose a staff member (if you have multiple branches), pick a time slot, and confirm without leaving the chat?
*   **Calendar Sync:** Does it instantly block that time on your Google Calendar so you don't get double-booked?
*   **Operational Logic:** Does it handle deposits, holiday schedules, and automated reminders?

waai offers a multi-branch booking system that lives entirely within WhatsApp. Your customers can browse services, see availability for specific staff members, and book in real-time. We offer two-way sync with Google Calendar, generate PDF receipts, and even handle deposit logic. It turns a chat thread into a functional point of sale.

## 3. Integrations and Data Ownership

Your WhatsApp automation shouldn’t live in a silo. It needs to talk to the tools you already use.

**Look for:**

*   **Contacts CRM:** Does the platform build a contact database for you? Can you segment those contacts and run broadcast campaigns later?
*   **Data Ownership:** This is critical. With some proprietary "black box" solutions, your conversation data is locked in their ecosystem. You need a platform that treats your data as your own.

waai integrates deeply with Google Workspace. Every appointment booked syncs to your Calendar; every customer inquiry updates your Contacts CRM. ## 4. AI Flexibility: Bring Your Own Model

"AI" is a buzzword, but not all AI is created equal. Some platforms lock you into a specific model or charge hidden premiums based on AI usage. Others might not allow you to tweak the temperature or system prompts, leaving you with a generic bot personality.

**Look for:**
*   **Model Choice:** Can you switch between OpenAI, Anthropic, or open-source models like Llama?
*   **Control:** Can you adjust the creativity (temperature) of the responses?
*   **Cost Control:** Does the platform allow you to use cheaper models for simple tasks and premium models for complex ones?

waai supports a "Bring Your Own AI" approach. We support OpenAI, Anthropic, z.ai, llama.cpp, and OpenRouter. This gives you the flexibility to choose the best model for your specific use case and budget, keeping you in control of your tech stack.

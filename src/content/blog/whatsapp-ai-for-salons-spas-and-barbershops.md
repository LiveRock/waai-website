## The Pain: No-Shows and the “After-Hours” Gap

Let’s be honest: the phone is a terrible booking tool for a modern service business. During peak hours, your front desk is swamped. Calls go to voicemail. Clients hang up and book with a competitor down the street. After hours, your shop is closed, but your clients’ lives aren’t.

**The No-Show Epidemic**

No-shows are the silent killer of salon profitability. Whether it’s a simple forgetfulness or a last-minute change of heart, an empty chair represents 100% lost revenue for that time slot. While traditional reminder SMS or emails help, they often go unread or ignored. WhatsApp, however, has open rates of nearly 98%. A message on WhatsApp feels personal and urgent.

**The 24/7 Expectation**

Consumers today are used to instant gratification. They want to book services immediately, not leave a voicemail and wait for a callback the next morning. If you aren't bookable 24/7, you are essentially turning away business. But hiring a 24/7 receptionist isn't financially viable for most independent salons or small chains.

You need a system that captures that intent the moment it strikes—whether that's at noon or midnight.

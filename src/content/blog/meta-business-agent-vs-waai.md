## What is Meta Business Agent?

Meta Business Agent is Meta's first-party AI assistant, baked into the WhatsApp Business app (and available across Instagram DMs and Messenger). After nearly two years of testing in India and Mexico, it launched globally on June 3, 2026, and over a million businesses were already using earlier versions at launch.

Out of the box, it can:

- Answer frequently asked customer questions
- Recommend products from your WhatsApp catalog
- Book basic calendar appointments
- Qualify sales leads
- Hand a conversation over to a human when it can't resolve a query

It learns from your business information automatically, requires no API integration, and is free to start — Meta monetizes it through WhatsApp Business Premium subscriptions for small businesses and token-based usage pricing for enterprises.

For a lot of small businesses, that's enough. For a lot of others, it isn't.

## What is waai?

Where Meta Business Agent is a single assistant inside an app, waai is a *platform*: you design conversations visually, connect them to your business systems, run a full booking operation, manage contacts, run broadcast campaigns, and pick your own AI model. And uniquely, waai is built to be **white-labeled and resold** — so agencies can offer "WhatsApp AI" to their entire client roster under their own brand.

## When Meta Business Agent is enough

We'll be straight with you — Meta's free agent is the right choice when **all** of these are true:

- ✅ You're a single small business with straightforward FAQ traffic
- ✅ "Book an appointment" means a basic calendar slot — no multiple branches, no staff scheduling, no deposits
- ✅ You don't need the bot to integrate with your CRM, calendar, or sheets
- ✅ You don't need custom conversation logic or branching
- ✅ You're not reselling WhatsApp AI to other businesses

If that's you, Meta Business Agent is genuinely great — and free. Go use it.

## First, the rules: how WhatsApp broadcasts actually work

WhatsApp isn't email. You can't just import a list and blast it. The WhatsApp Business API (which platforms like waai are built on) enforces a few ground rules:

**1. Opt-in is mandatory.** Every contact you message must have agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp. No bought lists, no scraped numbers.

**2. Marketing messages need templates.** To start a conversation with a customer (rather than reply to one), you send a pre-approved *message template*. Meta reviews these to keep quality high.

**3. The 24-hour window.** When a customer messages you, a 24-hour customer service window opens. Inside that window, you can reply freely. Outside it, you must use a template again. This is the single most misunderstood rule in WhatsApp marketing — and the reason your campaign tooling matters.

**4. Quality ratings are real.** Meta monitors how recipients respond to your messages. High block rates and low engagement degrade your number's standing, which affects how many messages you can send.

The good news: follow these rules and WhatsApp is arguably the highest-performing marketing channel available in 2026.

## Personalization and segmentation: stop sounding like a blast

Here's the uncomfortable truth about most WhatsApp marketing: people treat it like SMS spam, send generic blasts, and then wonder why they get blocked.

The fix is boring and effective:

- **Use names.** "Hi Sarah" beats "Dear customer" every time.
- **Segment before you send.** A salon shouldn't send the same message to someone who books haircuts monthly and someone who came once in 2024.
- **Match message type to segment.** New offer? Past buyers. Appointment reminder? Booked customers only. Re-engagement? People who've gone quiet.
- **Keep it conversational.** WhatsApp is a chat app. Short, human messages outperform corporate walls of text.

## Message templates and the 24-hour window, demystified

Let's make the template system concrete.

**When a customer messages you** (say, "Do you have availability Friday?"), the 24-hour window opens. Inside it, you — or your AI assistant — can reply freely: confirmations, answers, links, whatever the conversation needs.

**When the window closes**, that free-reply channel shuts. If you want to reach that customer again, you send an approved template — a booking reminder, a follow-up, a special offer.

This is why the best WhatsApp strategies don't treat campaigns as one-way blasts. They use templates to *open* conversations and automation to *carry* them:

1. Send a template campaign ("Your appointments for March are open — want to book?")
2. Customer replies
3. AI assistant or logic flow handles the reply: checks availability, books the slot, syncs your Google Calendar, sends a PDF receipt and reminder
4. You did nothing, and the customer got served in seconds

## A practical 2026 campaign playbook

Here's a simple, rule-compliant framework you can run this month:

**1. Build your opt-in list.** Add a WhatsApp contact button everywhere customers already are: website, invoices, booking confirmations, storefront.

**2. Segment ruthlessly.** Start with three: active customers, past customers, and leads who never converted.

**3. Write templates that sound human.** Lead with value, not "SALE!" A template like "Hi {{name}}, your next appointment is due around {{date}} — want me to grab you a slot?" will out-convert any ALL-CAPS promo.

**4. Send, then let automation take over.** Route replies to your AI assistant and booking flows so every campaign response is handled immediately.

**5. Watch quality signals.** Replies, opt-outs, and blocks are your report card. If blocks rise, your message or your segment is wrong — adjust before the next send.

**6. Repeat and refine.** Winning WhatsApp marketing is cadence plus relevance, not volume.

## FAQ

**Do my customers all need to save my number to receive a broadcast?**
No. On the WhatsApp Business API, you message customers directly from your own dedicated WhatsApp number — they don't need to have you saved. They do need to have opted in.

**What exactly is the 24-hour window?**
It opens when a customer messages you and closes 24 hours later. Inside it you can reply freely; outside it, any message you send must be an approved template. waai manages this for you so you don't have to track it manually.

**Can I send marketing messages to anyone on WhatsApp?**
No. Every recipient must have opted in to messages from your business, and your marketing content goes through template approval. Messaging people without opt-in is the fastest route to a blocked number.

**How is a broadcast different from a WhatsApp Group or Status?**
Groups and Status are informal and don't scale — no segmentation, no templates, no opt-in management, no CRM. API broadcasts are one-to-one messages delivered individually to each contact, measurable and compliant.

**Can my campaigns be personalized for each contact?**
Yes. With waai, templates pull in details from the contacts CRM, and segmentation controls who receives what. Every waai customer also gets their own WhatsApp number, so replies come back to an AI assistant that keeps the conversation going.

**How many messages can I send?**
Your number's messaging limits scale with its quality rating — send relevant, wanted messages and your capacity grows. Chasing volume with cold lists does the opposite.
